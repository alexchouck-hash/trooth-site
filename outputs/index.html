UI directory

185 dashboards, renderers, and output surfaces across 21 output types. Every one of them consumes at least one artifact Trooth already publishes, so none of them needs an adapter anybody has to maintain. Trooth is a pipe, and this is the list of things you can put on the end of it instead of building a front end.

The Trooth Console is the zero-setup default and needs no account. It is not the only option, and we would rather you used something you already run.

About the rating column

Trooth publishes no scores of any kind. Grades come from Modafied, which collects its own snapshots on its own clock and grades the sources Trooth routes to, plus MCP servers.

Modafied does not grade rendering tools, and the honest reason is that its six dimensions measure a publisher. Freshness lag is the grader's clock minus the source's own event time; revision behaviour, correction latency, completeness, schema stability, and internal consistency all describe data arriving over time. A charting library publishes no data, so there is nothing for those instruments to read.

So every tool here carries a rating slot that currently reads unrated, with the reason attached, rather than a number nobody measured. If Modafied takes rendering tools into its remit, these fill in from its published ratings with no change to this page.

What each output type is for

"Dashboard" covers at least five different jobs, and picking the wrong one is why most data projects end up with a chart nobody opens. The types are the useful unit of choice.

admin panel internal tool 11

Build a small internal app over a feed without writing a front end.

agent and chat surface 6

Where a human meets an agent that is reading Trooth. The envelope has to render here as provenance, not as a wall of JSON.

alerting and notification 8

Output that arrives rather than being visited. For change and revision feeds this is the real UI.

bi dashboard 13

Point-and-click dashboards over a dataset, built for people who do not write code.

charting library 14

Libraries for drawing the chart yourself, when you are embedding rather than adopting a tool.

chat and collaboration 7

Output that lands where the team already is. For most buyers this beats any page they have to remember to visit.

diagram and graph 8

For lineage, which is a graph and reads badly as a table.

email and digest 7

The scheduled summary. Unfashionable, and still the format a non-technical buyer forwards to their boss.

embeddable widget 7

Small marks that travel onto other people's pages. This is how the Modafied emblem spreads.

feed syndication 8

Machine-readable subscription formats. They cost nothing to publish and they outlive every UI on this list.

kiosk and signage 6

A screen on a wall that nobody clicks. Different constraints from a dashboard: no interaction, readable across a room, survives a reboot.

map and geo 10

Geographic output, needed by most of the government, transport, and environment feeds.

notebook and report 14

Narrative output where prose and computed figures live together and rebuild on a schedule.

print and pdf 6

A fixed document with a date on it. The regulatory and audit buyers ask for this first, whatever we would prefer.

realtime stream 7

For the sub-minute feeds where a page that refreshes every five minutes is useless.

spreadsheet 7

The output format most business buyers will actually open, whatever we would prefer.

sql and exploration 7

For the buyer who wants the archive, not a picture of it.

static site and docs 9

How the method, the catalog, and the cards get published at zero running cost.

status and uptime 9

The output shape for freshness and availability, which is most of what a status page is.

table and grid 11

Dense tabular output, which is what most people actually want from a catalog or a change feed.

terminal and cli 10

Output for operators and for agents that read text. Underrated and effectively free to support.

Trooth

The single data connector for apps and autonomous agents. Every fetch returns a cryptographically signed envelope with verifiable freshness, lineage, and audit records.
