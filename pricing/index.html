Our Economic & Architectural Commitments

No GPU / LLM in Hot Path

Routing is an ultra-fast, deterministic catalog lookup. We do not burn expensive tokens or introduce latency in the routing loop.

Hard $50/mo Cap (Rule H6)

We maintain a strict $50/month infrastructure cost ceiling during initial phases. Trooth operates on free edge tiers to ensure longevity.

No Sponsored Scores

Every score is mechanical. We never take money from providers to inflate scores or suppress lag metrics. Integrity is our entire brand.
