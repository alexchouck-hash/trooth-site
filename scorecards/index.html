Source Scorecards

Graded by Modafied

Every source Trooth routes to is graded across six mechanical dimensions. We do not do that grading, and we do not keep a copy of the result. The emblems below are fetched from Modafied as you load this page, so what you see is whatever the benchmark currently publishes.

Why we do not grade our own sources

Trooth sells the pipe these feeds travel through. A score published by the company selling you the pipe is discounted by every serious reader, usually to zero, so publishing one was worth less than not publishing one.

Modafied collects its own snapshots, on its own schedule, with its own clock. That last part is not a detail: freshness lag is the grader's clock minus the source's own event timestamp, so a grader working from somebody else's archive is measuring that supplier's scheduler rather than the source. Handing Modafied our snapshots would have made the independence claim false in the first respect anyone would check.

Trooth funds Modafied, and every Modafied page says so. Trooth's own MCP server will be graded there by the same code once it exists.

A feed reading insufficient data has not been withheld or hidden. It means the benchmark does not yet have the samples to grade it, and saying so rather than showing a confident number is the entire point. Read the published method, or see every connector we intend to support.
