Trooth has rated nothing, and that is the honest answer

All 2085 connectors read unrated in Trooth's own registry. The letter grades on this page come from Modafied, not from us: those emblems are fetched live from the benchmark and we keep no copy of them. Trooth's own method has not produced a grade for two real reasons, both being fixed before any Trooth grade appears here:

  • The scoring job still returns fixed constants where measurements belong. A grade computed from those would be decoration.
  • The archive does not yet cover a long enough window for the percentiles to mean anything.

Publishing a confident letter grade of our own today would be the exact failure this project exists to argue against. The count of connectors Trooth has rated is the real progress bar, and it is allowed to sit at zero while an independent benchmark that we fund, but do not control, grades the same feeds in public.

A cell reading insufficient data means the dimension has fewer samples than the method requires. It is never drawn as a zero, because zero is a measurement and this is the absence of one.

How the emblem reads

Six bars, one per dimension, always shown. A letter grade appears only when all six are measured, and it comes from a published equal-weighted formula rather than a private one. A dotted border means provisional: measured, but over a window shorter than the method requires. Grey bars mean no data. MCP servers are graded by Modafied, not by Trooth, because Trooth is itself an MCP server.
