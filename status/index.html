Known, and not fixed yet

  • The snapshot archive is not persisting. The scheduled job writes to the runner's disk and the runner is then destroyed, so no history accumulates. Until that is fixed, replay and the derived feeds it would support do not exist.
  • Modafied's manifest does not match the files it lists. 5 of its published ratings fail our integrity check, because the manifest describes an earlier generation of its own artifacts. That is a publisher bug rather than an attack. The grades below are shown with that caveat and are not used for routing.
