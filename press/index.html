Press and pitch assets

Silent, looping, animated decks. Built for LinkedIn, email signatures and direct messages, and posted here so a link is always available.

Each one is a 16:9 GIF of about 40 seconds. They loop, and they are built to make sense entered at any frame. Click a poster to play it.

What is shipped, and what these describe

These decks describe the connector in the present tense. It is specified and not yet built. Trooth is at Phase 0: five sources are merged into the routing catalog, snapshots and diffs run on a schedule, and the site and Console are live. The REST API, the MCP server and the signed envelope are Phase 1 and are gated.

The roadmap says what has to clear before each phase starts, and the status page says what is running right now. Read those before quoting a deck.

Investor decks

Written for a different reader, and posted here rather than attached to an email. They argue a structure and a sequence, not a shipped product. The ask card is cut from both, because the size of a raise is not a public asset. What is left includes gate targets rather than measurements, and each frame that shows one says so on the frame.
